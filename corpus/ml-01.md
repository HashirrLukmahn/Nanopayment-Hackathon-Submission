## Abstract

We report systematic reproducibility failures when fine-tuning BERT-base-uncased on GLUE subtasks (MNLI, QQP, SST-2) at learning rate 3e-4, a value we observed in several community HuggingFace notebooks without provenance. Across 50 seeds, final validation accuracy standard deviation was 7.8 percentage points on MNLI — dramatically worse than the 0.5 pp reported by the original BERT authors at their recommended 2e-5 learning rate. Half of our runs diverged to chance performance. This document characterizes the failure mode and offers a diagnostic.

## Methods

Fine-tuning used HuggingFace Transformers 4.35.0, PyTorch 2.1.0, CUDA 12.1. Hardware: 8× NVIDIA A100 80GB. Batch size 32, max sequence length 128. AdamW optimizer, weight decay 0.01, linear warmup over 10% of training, 3 epochs. 50 random seeds per configuration (0–49).

We compared four learning rates: 1e-5, 2e-5, 5e-5, 3e-4. All other hyperparameters held fixed. MNLI (393k train, 9.8k dev matched), QQP (364k train, 40k dev), SST-2 (67k train, 872 dev).

## Results

MNLI dev-matched accuracy (mean ± std across 50 seeds):
- LR 1e-5: 83.1 ± 0.4
- LR 2e-5: 84.6 ± 0.3
- LR 5e-5: 84.8 ± 0.6
- LR 3e-4: 41.9 ± 7.8 — 23/50 seeds at random chance

SST-2:
- LR 1e-5: 91.2 ± 0.5
- LR 2e-5: 92.5 ± 0.4
- LR 5e-5: 92.3 ± 0.5
- LR 3e-4: 68.4 ± 11.2 — 18/50 seeds at chance

QQP showed similar patterns at LR 3e-4 (mean F1 62.1 ± 9.4 with 20/50 seed divergences).

Divergent runs exhibited gradient explosion at steps 200-500, followed by a permanent loss-plateau at the chance-level cross-entropy.

## Discussion

Layer-wise learning rate analysis revealed the failure is driven by early-epoch destabilization of layer norm parameters in lower BERT layers, not the classifier head. At 3e-4, layer norm gamma/beta values jump by O(1) in the first 100 steps — an order of magnitude above the successful LR 2e-5 regime. Once displaced, these parameters rarely recover even with the full training budget.

We believe some community notebooks were inadvertently copied from RoBERTa recipes, where 3e-4 is more defensible given the different pre-training dynamics. For BERT-base, our recommendation is 2e-5 ± 1e-5 as the safe range, with careful monitoring of layer norm drift if higher LRs are required for specific tasks.

Practical diagnostic: plot ||γ||, ||β|| per layer vs step during the first 500 training steps. A >2× jump in the first 100 steps is a strong predictor of divergence at that seed.
