## Abstract

A 14-month PDE4B inhibitor discovery effort was terminated after systematic hERG patch-clamp measurements across 42 analogues placed the best compounds at hERG IC50 between 0.3 and 1.1 μM — unacceptable given projected free Cmax of 0.5 μM for efficacy. Attempts to dial down the basic nitrogen pKa succeeded on the hERG axis but destroyed PDE4B potency. This document records the pharmacophore conflict and the three structural rescues we attempted.

## Methods

hERG was measured by automated patch-clamp (Qube 384) using HEK293 cells stably expressing hERG at room temperature and physiological [K+]. Compounds were tested at 7 concentrations (0.03–30 μM), three replicates per concentration. Dofetilide (IC50 = 10 nM) was the positive control.

PDE4B enzymatic assays used recombinant human PDE4B1 at 0.5 nM, cAMP substrate at Km = 1 μM, AlphaScreen readout. Cell-based potency was measured in THP-1 cells stimulated with LPS (100 ng/mL, 6 h), TNFα by ELISA.

## Results

Across 42 analogues, mean hERG IC50 = 1.4 μM (range 0.3–6.8 μM). PDE4B enzymatic IC50 was < 10 nM for 18 compounds. Of those 18, 16 had hERG IC50 < 1 μM — a strong negative correlation.

The basic piperazine nitrogen at the 4-position of the hinge-binding aryl was identified as the primary hERG driver: pKa reduction from 8.9 to 6.2 (via fluorination adjacent to the basic center) reduced hERG inhibition from 0.4 μM to 8.3 μM but also reduced PDE4B potency from 4 nM to 280 nM. Replacing the piperazine with morpholine dropped hERG to >20 μM but also dropped PDE4B potency below 1 μM.

Structural analyses (crystal soak of analogue PDE4B-024 at 1.8 Å) showed the basic nitrogen forms a salt bridge with D382 of the PDE4B catalytic domain — the same feature is apparently critical for hERG pore binding through an analogous electrostatic interaction.

## Discussion

The pharmacophore conflict is fundamental: the PDE4B-active basic nitrogen is the same feature binding hERG. Three rescues we attempted: (1) bioisosteric replacement with tetrazole — lost 80% potency; (2) intramolecular H-bond "hiding" of the basic center — marginal hERG improvement (~3×); (3) full chemotype switch to a neutral quinazoline core — hERG cleared but PDE4B selectivity over PDE4A/C/D collapsed.

We concluded that the optimal dataset for teams working on PDE4 is published in the open literature up through 2016 and then a wall of negative results. Our negative data expands the wall in one sector. Groups with neutral pharmacophores (benzofuran, pyrazolopyrimidine) should not port directly from our data — the electrostatic dependence we observed may not apply.
