## Abstract

The BRF-119 series (BRF-119-A through BRF-119-J) was designed as a set of back-ups to the primary clinical candidate for a Mcl-1 inhibitor program. During in vitro DDI profiling we observed CYP3A4 IC50 values of 0.4–2.1 μM across the series, with no analogue clearing the 10 μM screening threshold. Combined with the projected free Cmax of 0.8–1.4 μM in a first-in-human scenario, the DDI risk rendered the entire series untenable. We discontinued the chemistry optimization program and redirected resources.

## Methods

CYP3A4 inhibition was measured using the fluorogenic midazolam 1'-hydroxylation assay in pooled human liver microsomes at substrate concentration = 2 μM (Km), inhibitor concentrations = 0.1, 0.3, 1, 3, 10, 30 μM. Time-dependent inhibition was evaluated using a 30-min NADPH preincubation. Ketoconazole served as a positive control (IC50 = 0.02 μM consistent with published values).

Parallel assessment was done for CYP1A2, CYP2C9, CYP2C19, CYP2D6, CYP3A4 (with testosterone as alternate substrate), and CYP2B6. All compounds were tested in two independent runs with three technical replicates.

## Results

BRF-119-A: CYP3A4 IC50 = 0.4 μM (midazolam), 0.9 μM (testosterone). 
BRF-119-B: 0.8 μM / 1.4 μM. 
BRF-119-C: 1.1 μM / 2.3 μM. 
BRF-119-D: 0.7 μM / 1.6 μM. 
BRF-119-E: 1.4 μM / 3.1 μM. 
BRF-119-F: 2.1 μM / 4.8 μM. 
BRF-119-G: 0.6 μM / 1.2 μM. 
BRF-119-H: 0.9 μM / 1.9 μM. 
BRF-119-I: 1.8 μM / 3.6 μM. 
BRF-119-J: 1.3 μM / 2.7 μM.

Time-dependent inhibition (TDI) signals were strongest for BRF-119-A and BRF-119-G (kinact/KI > 0.1 min⁻¹·μM⁻¹) — consistent with metabolism-dependent inhibition via the terminal morpholine warhead. The pyrrolo-pyrimidine core common to the series appears to be the primary liability for reversible inhibition.

## Discussion

Three remediation strategies were evaluated: (1) modification of the morpholine to a piperazine abolished CYP3A4 inhibition but dropped Mcl-1 binding by 30-fold; (2) installation of a 4-methyl substituent on the morpholine partially rescued CYP3A4 (IC50 → 5.4 μM) but with a 6-fold potency loss; (3) repositioning the hinge-binder nitrogen broke the series' SAR entirely.

We conclude that the pyrrolo-pyrimidine / morpholine combination is simultaneously the binding pharmacophore and the DDI liability — a textbook "same atom is doing two jobs" failure mode. Teams considering similar scaffolds should front-load CYP inhibition measurement at the hit-to-lead stage rather than waiting for lead optimization; the structural dependency is unlikely to be decoupled by late-stage tweaks.
